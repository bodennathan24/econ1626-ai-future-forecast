AI and the Future of Australian Real Estate by 2030

Thesis

By 2030, artificial intelligence will reshape Australia’s real estate industry by automating routine agent work, improving property search and valuation, and giving large platforms and investors stronger data advantages. While this could increase productivity and reduce transaction costs, it may also worsen information inequality for ordinary buyers and renters unless policy improves transparency, privacy, and fairness in AI-driven property decisions.

Project Overview

This interactive web essay will explore how AI capabilities may affect property search, valuation, real estate agents, investors, renters, buyers, and regulators in Australia by 2030.
